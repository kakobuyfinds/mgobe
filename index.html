From spreadsheet find to order

1. Match the listing

Keep the item ID with the product name. On HipobuyFind, confirm the listing and check whether the displayed price applies to the size and color you want. Compare garment measurements against something you already wear.

2. Review the QC photos

If your agent offers warehouse quality-control photos, check the selected color, size label, stitching and visible marks. Ask for a measurement photo when fit matters, and check the return deadline before approving the parcel.

3. Estimate the delivered cost

Add the selected variant price, domestic delivery, agent fees and international shipping. Include applicable taxes or duties, and check whether the route bills by actual or volumetric weight.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that organizes product finds for comparison. This independent guide shows a selection from HipobuyFind with item IDs and listed prices.

Can I order any listed item through KakoBuy?

Check the original marketplace link and the selected variant first. If you plan to use KakoBuy, confirm that it accepts that link and can purchase the item before paying.

Why might the seller price differ from the spreadsheet?

A listing can show a starting price while particular sizes or colors cost more. Sellers can also change prices and stock after a catalog entry is captured. Use the selected variant price when estimating your order total.
